What you get

Fast to load, safe to run

  • Instant start. Audio is cached, so pads open at once every time you launch.
  • Pro audio output. ASIO, WASAPI or DirectSound, stereo or mono, master level and fade-out.
  • Three pad modes. Play, Stutter (restarts on each press) and Hold (plays while pressed).
  • Banks. Sort hundreds of sounds into tabs and keep playing while you switch.
  • Drag and drop. Drop files or a whole folder and each file becomes a pad.
  • Trim each pad. Start and end cue, gain, loop, color, hotkey and MIDI learn.
  • Show lock. Lock Edit mode so nothing moves or gets deleted mid-event.
  • Loudness normalize. Soft, Medium or Loud, so every track plays at the same level.
  • Grid or list view, full screen, dark or light theme.
  • Sessions. Save one file per event and reopen it any time.
  • Your own brand. Licensed copies can show your name or logo in the header.
  • Keyboard sizing. Ctrl+ and Ctrl- change pad size, Space stops everything.
